# Estimating: Automated Claims Processing

## Initiative
Enable policyholders to submit and track claims digitally, with automated validation and routing to reduce manual review time and accelerate payouts.

---

## Epics

### Epic 1: Digital Claims Submission
Enable policyholders to submit claims online or via mobile with document and photo upload capabilities.

**Key features:**
- Online and mobile claim submission form
- Document and photo upload
- Auto-save and resume incomplete submissions
- Confirmation and reference number generation

**Estimate:** `[40 70%]` → Adjusted (90th %): ~65 story points

---

### Epic 2: Claims Status & Notifications
Provide policyholders with real-time visibility into the status of their claim and automated updates throughout the process.

**Key features:**
- Real-time claims status tracking
- Automated email and SMS notifications at each stage
- Claims history dashboard for policyholders
- Estimated resolution timeline

**Estimate:** `[25 75%]` → Adjusted (90th %): ~38 story points

---

### Epic 3: Automated Claims Validation & Routing
Automatically validate submitted claims for completeness and route them to the appropriate adjuster or processing queue.

**Key features:**
- Automated completeness checks on submission
- Fraud detection and flagging
- Rules-based routing to appropriate adjuster or team
- Straight-through processing for simple/low-value claims

**Estimate:** `[60 50%]` → Adjusted (90th %): ~120 story points

---

### Epic 4: Adjuster Workflow Management
Provide claims adjusters with digital tools to efficiently review, communicate, and resolve assigned claims.

**Key features:**
- Adjuster dashboard with assigned claims queue
- In-app messaging with policyholders
- Document review and annotation tools
- Decision recording and audit trail

**Estimate:** `[50 60%]` → Adjusted (90th %): ~90 story points

---

### Epic 5: Payment Processing Integration
Automate payment initiation and tracking once a claim has been approved, reducing payout time.

**Key features:**
- Integration with payment processing system
- Automated payment initiation on claim approval
- Multiple payment method support (direct deposit, check, etc.)
- Payment status tracking and confirmation notifications

**Estimate:** `[45 55%]` → Adjusted (90th %): ~85 story points
