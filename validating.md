# Validating Exercise: Automated Claims Processing

## Initiative
Enable policyholders to submit and track claims digitally, with automated validation and routing to reduce manual review time and accelerate payouts.

## Validation Techniques Used
- Internal Customer Analytics
- Surveying Customers
- Competitive Analysis
- User Testing

## Validation Plan
Shared with: product team, claims adjusters, and customer experience stakeholders.

## Questions by Technique

**Internal Analytics:**
- What is the average claims processing time?
- What % of claims require manual intervention?

**Surveying Customers:**
- How satisfied are you with the current claims process?
- What is the biggest frustration when filing a claim?

**Competitive Analysis:**
- Which insurers offer fully digital claims?
- What features do they include?

**User Testing:**
- Can users successfully submit a claim without assistance?
- Where do users get confused in the flow?

## Results

**Internal Analytics:**
- Average claims processing time: 14 days
- 73% of claims require manual intervention
- Peak submission times are Monday mornings, causing bottlenecks

**Surveying Customers:**
- 68% of customers rated the claims process as "difficult" or "very difficult"
- Top frustrations: lack of status updates (82%), too much paperwork (74%), long wait times (69%)
- 91% said they would prefer a digital option

**Competitive Analysis:**
- Lemonade processes some claims in under 3 minutes via AI
- State Farm and Allstate both offer mobile claims submission
- Key features competitors offer: photo/document upload, real-time status tracking, instant messaging with adjusters

**User Testing:**
- 8 out of 10 users successfully submitted a test claim without assistance
- Main confusion point: users unsure which documents to upload
- Average completion time: 6 minutes

## Outcome
All validation techniques returned strong positive signals. Value and confidence in this initiative are **up**.

## Consensus

**Value: 8/10**
- Strong customer demand (91% want digital option)
- Clear competitive pressure (major insurers already have this)
- Significant operational inefficiency to fix (73% manual intervention)

**Value Confidence: 8/10**
- Multiple validation techniques all pointed in the same direction
- Real data from internal analytics backs it up
- User testing showed high success rate

**Status update: move initiative to `estimating`**
